How it works
Three steps, and your team doesn't have to learn anything new.

  

    
1
A key is generated
It's created on your device or HSM module and never leaves your control.
prot keys create --size 256

    
2
Your data is encrypted
Files and messages are protected before they leave your computer.
prot encrypt report.pdf

    
3
Keys are safeguarded
Rotate keys, audit activity and revoke access from one dashboard.
prot keys rotate --all

Frequently asked questions



  
Can prot read my files?
No. Encryption happens on your computer and the keys are yours. Without them, nobody can open the data, not even us.

  
What happens if I lose a device?
The data stays encrypted. You can also revoke its keys from the dashboard so it stops working with your account.

  
How long does setup take?
Cloud services are active within minutes. Devices arrive pre-configured with a quick-start guide.

  
Can I combine services and devices?
Yes, and we recommend it. The calculator on this page shows an example of a combined plan.
